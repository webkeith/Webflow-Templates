MEET JESUS

Jesus is alive. His invitation is for you.

We believe in one God—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. They are three distinct Persons, fully and equally God, sharing one divine being.

Jesus is the eternal Son of God who became truly human. He entered our world, lived without sin, and gave His life on the cross for us.

He rose bodily from the dead. He is alive today.

Through Jesus, we receive forgiveness and new life by grace through faith. You can come to Him honestly, with your need and your questions.

If you are wondering who He is or what following Him means, start here. We would love to help you take the next step.
